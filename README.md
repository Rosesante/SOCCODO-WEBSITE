# SOCCODO Website

**Social, Climate and Community Development Organization**

A modern, responsive website designed to present SOCCODO's mission, programs, community projects, activities, and impact. The website provides an online platform where visitors can learn about the organization, explore its initiatives, view project photographs, and find contact information.

## About SOCCODO

SOCCODO is a community development organization focused on improving the lives of vulnerable and rural communities through social development, education, environmental initiatives, disaster relief, water source restoration, and community empowerment.

The website aims to increase the organization's online visibility, showcase its work, and encourage collaboration with individuals, volunteers, and development partners.

## Features

- **Home:** Introduction to SOCCODO and its activities.
- **About Us:** Organizational background, mission, vision, and values.
- **Programs:** Overview of the organization's development initiatives.
- **Projects:** Showcase of community projects and activities.
- **News:** Updates, announcements, and community stories.
- **Gallery:** Photographs of projects and organizational activities.
- **Contact Us:** Contact information and a form for visitor inquiries.

## Programs and Areas of Focus

- Community Development
- Education Support
- Climate and Environmental Conservation
- Social Services
- Disaster Relief
- Water and Sanitation

## Technology Stack

The frontend is built using:

- **React** — User interface development.
- **Vite** — Development server and build tool.
- **JavaScript** — Website functionality.
- **CSS** — Styling and responsive layouts.
- **HTML5** — Website structure.
